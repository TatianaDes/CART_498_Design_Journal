### Goal of the Prototype Week 6
The goal of my prototype is to have the user be able to navigate the performer's side and the audience's side of the application, and see if it is easy to navigate and find all information. I am hoping to see if they can do all these steps in the performer's side:
1. Toggle to the performer's side of the application.
2. Make a profile. (Coming Soon)
3. Find themselves on the map by neighbourhood or venue.
4. Drop a pin.
5. Set a timer.
6. Add a title and genre.
7. Publish the performance.
8. See the timer.
9. See the tip jar.
10. Extend the performance.
11. End the set.
12. Use the application without WiFi.
13. Start over for a new performance.
14. Use settings to change appearances.

I am hoping to see if they can do all these steps in the audience's side:
1. Toggle the audience's side of the application.
2. See artist profiles. (Coming Soon)
3. Find artist by name or venue.
4. See who is playing and hear a sample
5. Get directions.
6. Give tips through the tip jar with money directly from phone.
7. Use settings to change appearances.
8. Use settings to change notifications.


My goal is to hopefully see where the weakest points are at the moment with my prototype, so that my next prototype cleans up and fixes the elements people were struggling with.
