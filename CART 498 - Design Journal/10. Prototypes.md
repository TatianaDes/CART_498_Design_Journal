### Goal of the Prototype Week 9
The goal of my prototype is to have the user be able to navigate the performer's side and the audience's side of the application, and see if it is easy to navigate and find all information. What I hope to see is:
1. See if the colours have improved.
2. See if navigation is understandable and easy in both A and B
3. See if visually the website helps guide the viewer through the space.
4. See if I have improved more as time has gone on.

My goal is to hopefully see where the weakest points are at the moment with my prototype, so that my next prototype cleans up and fixes the elements people were struggling with.
