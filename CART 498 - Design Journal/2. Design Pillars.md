## Design Pillars Week 6
1. Usability - Can Users Accomplish Goals Efficiently
It is really important for users to come onto this application and easily be able to navigate it, whether they are a performer or an audience member.
For performers, they need:
+ A profile, with a small profile with their name, genre of music and a small sample of their music, their audience can find them and follow them to get live updates. (Coming Soon)
+ To easily be able to find available outdoor spaces and venues that they can easily click on the available locations, set up their performance title, time, genre and publish for the audience to see.
+ Once live, everything is either changing on its own; such as the live countdown timer continuously going down, and the live tip jar continuously increasing, or one click of a button, such as the 15 minute extend set button and the end set button.
+ Once they are done their performance, they can start the cycle all over again with simple venue and home buttons.

For the audience, they need:
+ To be able to find performers by looking up their name, or finding the outdoor venue they are performing at.
+ They can also find performers and then follow their profile to be able to get updates and messages about the performers they follow. (Coming Soon)
+ See how much time is left of their performance.
+ Possibly listen to the samples of the artists before choosing to go to their performance or not.
+ Get directions and go straight to the performance.
+ Tip the performer if they want to by Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal.

Both performer and audience member can easily navigate the website by using the navigation bar, the prompted questions, and the informative buttons.

3. Findability - Can Users Locate What They Need
While the performer and audience side of the application are different in terms of goals and uses, they have similar layouts to make sure all screens are easy to navigate and clear.
Here is what is in the app to make it more easier to navigate:
+ The navigation bar has all the different locations each user can navigate to on the app.
+ All buttons are large and very contrasted to the background for easier visibility.
+ All drop downs and filled out containers have a title stating what each of them represent.
+ All text is kept short and concise for quick reading.

5.  Accessibility - Can Everyone Use It?
Accessibility is very important on this application, and that is why:
+ The buttons are contrasted from the background and large enough in text and button size to be seen.
+ The buttons change colour for a brief second after being pressed to give the user feedback of which button was pressed.
+ When important questions are asked that can lead to potential unwanted publication or going live, the questions are in large text and contrasted with the background.
+ When the navigation bar is pressed, the location of the user changes to a redish-pink colour so the user is aware of what page they are one.
+ In the more screen, the user can change the application to be in light or dark mode, as well as if they want to have blue light or not.
