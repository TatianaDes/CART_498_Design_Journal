## Detailed Features Week 6

### Hypotheses
1. Fix headings to blend in to the background less.
At the moment the heading colour is too similar to the background and blends in, it should either be lighter or be taken away on other screens.

2. Pop-up bubbles should either be gone for a cleaner look, or stay but change colours to not blend in the the background.
For a more modern look, the pop-up bubbles should not be there unless they make sure the text does not blend in to the background, and for the ones that are necessary, they should be of a lighter colour so that they do not blend in to the background. 

3. Add a profile navigation bar option, where performers can create their profile with a picture, a description of themselves, their genre of music, and a sample of their music. (Coming Soon)
It would be a big improvement to the application if performers were able to create a profile for themselves and see their followers and know that their audience is able to see them. It would also be a great improvement for communication, where performers could post that they are live and their audience can get notified.

4. Audience can read these profiles and follow to get live notifications. (Coming Soon)
The audience would have another icon in their navigation bar where they can follow performers by looking them up and seeing their profiles. Once they follow them, when an artist posts, the audience will know immediately by a notification.

5. Performers can name their performance.
Performers can give a name to their set to feel more inviting and welcoming to new audience members or it can be informative where it states who is featured.

6. Performer's can add a genre to the publishing page with a dropdown.
Adding a dropdown for selecting what genre the performance will be before publishing will be very beneficial for the audience to know what kind of music they may be hearing quicker than having to listen to the sample first.

7. The pop-up for the audience when they are trying to find an artist should now include what the artist named their performance (maybe make it on a new page).
The pop-up where the audience selects a pin and can see the artist and get a bit of information, should now include the what the performer named their set. Either the pop-up should be larger to be able to see it better, or be on a completely new page. 

8. Tip jar should have a pay symbol like Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal.
Having a symbol for the pay on the tip jar screen will allow the audience to know they can make fast payments with whatever card they may have already in their phone.

9. The audience's notifications should now have a toggle for allowing notifications from followed artists.
Audience member's will have control over if they want notifications from their favourite artists or not with a toggle they can turn on and off in the settings.
